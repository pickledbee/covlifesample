What a Sunday looks like

Simple, reverent, and centered on the Bible

Our worship follows a pattern Presbyterians have used for centuries. We read Scripture, pray, sing psalms and hymns together, and hear a sermon that walks through a passage of the Bible. On communion Sundays we celebrate the Lord's Supper together.

  1. Call to worship and opening hymn
  2. Confession of sin and assurance of pardon
  3. Scripture readings and prayer
  4. Sermon from the Bible
  5. Closing hymn and benediction

Common questions

Before you come

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best. Come as you are.

Where do I park, and which door do I use?

Parking is on site at the church. Use the main front entrance and someone will be there to greet you. Add specific parking details

What is Sunday School like?

Sunday School at 9:30 AM is a relaxed Bible class for adults. Come early with your coffee, bring your questions, and join the conversation, or just listen.

Can I bring my children?

Of course. Children are always welcome to sit with their families during worship, and a little noise is perfectly fine.

Will I be asked to stand up or introduce myself?

No. You won't be singled out. We'll simply be glad you came, and if you'd like to talk afterward, we'd love that too.

Do I need to bring a Bible?

You're welcome to, but there are Bibles in the pews, and we'll give you a free one to keep if you'd like.

Can I take communion?

The Lord's Supper is for baptized believers who are members in good standing of a Bible-believing church. The pastor explains this before communion each time, and you're always welcome to simply watch and pray.

Is there an offering? Do I have to give?

Guests are never expected to give. The offering is for members and regular attenders as part of their worship.

Is the building accessible?

Let us know if you need help getting in or getting a seat, and we'll gladly have someone meet you. Confirm accessibility details

Let us know you're coming

We'll save you a seat

Totally optional. Tell us you're planning to visit and someone will be watching for you at the door. You can also ask for a free Bible, a visit from the pastor, or just ask a question.

What we believe

Reformed and Presbyterian, in plain words

The Bible is our final authority in everything we believe and do. These are the heart of it.

The Bible

Scripture is God's Word, completely true and trustworthy. It is the only rule for what we believe and how we live.

One God, three persons

We worship one God in three persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

Saved by grace

We are saved by God's grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. Salvation is God's gift, not something we earn.

Jesus Christ

Jesus is fully God and fully man. He died for sinners, rose from the dead, and will come again.

"Reformed"

We hold to the teaching recovered in the Protestant Reformation, which puts God's glory and grace at the center.

"Presbyterian"

Our church is led by elders, and we belong to a wider family of churches who hold one another accountable.

Our doctrine is summarized in the Westminster Confession of Faith and the Larger and Shorter Catechisms. We are a congregation of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church.
